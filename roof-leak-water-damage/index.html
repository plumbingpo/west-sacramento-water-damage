Roof Leak Water Damage Help in West Sacramento

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. Rain slips under a lifted shingle, a cracked flashing joint or a tired skylight seal, then runs along the decking and rafters before it drops onto the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Right Now

What to Do Right Now

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Catch the DripsFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Photograph the DamageTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
What Is at Risk

Where the Damage Spreads

Roof Framing

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Once they rot, the frame supporting the roof is weaker.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Leak water heads for openings in the ceiling, such as recessed lights, fan boxes and junction boxes. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Insulation in the Attic

Once soaked, insulation flattens, loses its value and keeps the drywall under it damp for days. Drying is sometimes possible with fiberglass. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Mold in the Attic

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours and spread across the underside of the decking.

Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Pricing

How the Quote Is Built

  • How easy the attic is to reach, how high the ceilings are, and if the roof needs a tarp before work begins.
  • The length of time the leak went unnoticed, plus whether mold has started.
  • Whether wet attic insulation can be dried in place, and how large an area it covers.
  • How much repainting is required so the patched ceiling blends in.

Trace the Leak Path
