Finding Your Water Shutoff Valve in West Sacramento, CA

Home / Blog / Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in West Sacramento, CA

Stopping the flow of water is the quickest way to limit damage during a water emergency. You could avoid extensive property damage and save thousands of dollars just by knowing where your shutoff valve is and how it works. If you own a home in West Sacramento, CA, find your shutoff valve and practice using it now, not during a leak.

Finding the Main Shutoff Valve in Your Home

In most West Sacramento homes, the main water shutoff valve is located in one of these areas: near the front of the house where the water line enters from the street, in the garage along an interior wall, in the basement or crawl space near the foundation wall, in a utility room or laundry area, or in a ground-level access box near the street (the meter shutoff). Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. Ball valves are more reliable and can be turned off with a quarter turn.

How to Shut Off Water Quickly

A gate valve closes when you turn its handle clockwise, to the right, for several full turns, to the point where it will not go further. With a ball valve, swing the lever until it is perpendicular to the pipe. If the valve is stuck, do not force it, because you may break it and make the situation worse. Go to the meter and close it there with a meter key or an adjustable wrench.

Shutoff Valves at Each Fixture

Most fixtures and appliances have their own shutoff valves that allow you to stop water to that specific location without shutting off the entire house. Look for shutoff valves under sinks, behind toilets, behind washing machines, and near water heaters. Learn where each one is so you can quickly stop a leak in one place and still have water everywhere else in the house.
