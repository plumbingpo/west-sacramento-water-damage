Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
