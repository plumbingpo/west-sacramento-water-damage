A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. For flood emergencies anywhere in West Sacramento, West Sacramento Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
